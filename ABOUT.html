About Shawn Lee

Shawn Lee (born 1963) is an American musician, producer, composer, and multi-instrumentalist. He is the co-founder of the London-based soft rock duo Young Gun Silver Fox, alongside Andy Platts. Shawn is best known for creating the entire score for the acclaimed video game Bully by Rockstar Games. His song, Kiss the Sky, was featured in the Telltale video game Tales from the Borderlands. Currently based in London, Shawn is working on an upcoming album under Janktone Productions, composed primarily of cheap and fun home-made instruments. There is no set release date yet.

Early Life and Career Beginnings

Shawn Lee was born to a mother of Lebanese and American Indian ancestry and a father of Irish American ancestry. In pursuit of his career, Shawn relocated twice. He moved to Los Angeles in 1988, where he worked with legendary producers The Dust Brothers and collaborated with the iconic musician Jeff Buckley. After performing on The Tonight Show, securing a publishing deal with EMI, and touring with Lollapalooza, Shawn moved to London, England. There, he signed a solo record deal with Talkin' Loud and released his debut album, Discomfort, in 1996.

Musical Achievements

Shawn's career spans over three decades, during which he has composed music for films like Ocean's Thirteen, The Break-Up, and Confessions of a Shopaholic, as well as TV series such as CSI: Miami, Lost, and Desperate Housewives. His work with the Ping Pong Orchestra has produced 11 albums, blending genres like funk, soul, and psychedelia. Shawn also scored the surf documentary Under the Sun and the yet-to-be-released UK/Swiss feature film Save Angel Hope.

Video Game Scores

Shawn's talent for creating immersive soundscapes has made him a prominent figure in the world of video game scores. In addition to Bully, he composed music for the Rockstar Games video game The Getaway. His work on Bully earned him two nominations for best score from GameSpot (winner) and Spike VGA (Video Game Awards).

Collaborations

Shawn has collaborated with a wide range of artists, including Lana Del Rey, Amy Winehouse, Alicia Keys, UNKLE, and Coldcut. His ability to blend genres and experiment with unique sounds has made him a sought-after composer and producer in the music industry. In 2011, Shawn collaborated with indie artist AM, releasing their debut album Celestial Electric to critical acclaim. The duo's first single, Dark Into Light, was released on limited edition 12" vinyl in honor of Record Store Day.

Young Gun Silver Fox

In addition to his solo work, Shawn is the co-founder of the soft rock duo Young Gun Silver Fox with Andy Platts. They have released four albums: West End Coast (2015), AM Waves (2018), Canyons (2020), and Ticket to Shangri-La (2022). The duo's music is inspired by the smooth sounds of the 1970s and 1980s, combining lush harmonies, catchy melodies, and intricate production.

Current Projects

Shawn continues to live in London, working on new projects in his studio, Trans-Yank. He is currently collaborating with artists like Clutchy Hopkins, Tony Joe White, and Tommy Guerrero. His upcoming album under Janktone Productions promises to be a unique blend of home-made instruments and innovative sounds, showcasing his creativity and passion for music.

Discography

Shawn Lee's extensive discography includes solo albums such as Discomfort (1996), Monkey Boy (2000), and Soul Visa (2004). As Shawn Lee's Ping Pong Orchestra, he has released 11 albums, including Music And Rhythm (2004), Moods And Grooves (2005), and World Of Funk (2011). His work with Young Gun Silver Fox includes albums like West End Coast (2015) and Ticket to Shangri-La (2022).
