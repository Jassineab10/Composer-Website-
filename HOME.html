Shawn Lee

Shawn Lee (born 1963) is an American musician, producer, composer, and multi-instrumentalist, best known for creating the entire score for the acclaimed video game Bully by Rockstar Games. With a career spanning over three decades, Shawn has composed music for films like Ocean's Thirteen and The Break-Up, as well as TV series such as CSI: Miami and Lost. His song "Kiss the Sky" was featured in the Telltale video game Tales from the Borderlands. Currently based in London, Shawn is working on an upcoming album under Janktone Productions, composed primarily of cheap and fun home-made instruments. Born to a multicultural family, Shawn has lived in Los Angeles and London, collaborating with legends like Jeff Buckley, The Dust Brothers, and Amy Winehouse.

As the co-founder of the soft rock duo Young Gun Silver Fox, Shawn has released four albums, including West End Coast and Ticket to Shangri-La. His work with the Ping Pong Orchestra has produced 11 albums, blending genres like funk, soul, and psychedelia. Shawn's music has also appeared in snowboard films, surf documentaries, and art exhibitions. A prolific collaborator, he has worked with artists like Lana Del Rey, Alicia Keys, and UNKLE.
